FAQ

Common questions

Have another question? Just ask me.

Which platforms do you work with?

Mainly WordPress (Elementor Pro, Divi, Gutenberg, WPBakery), WooCommerce and Shopify. I also work with GoHighLevel, Wix and Squarespace.

Can you build my website from a Figma design?

Yes. Figma to WordPress is one of my core services. Send me your Figma, XD or PSD file and I'll build a pixel-perfect, responsive website from it.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Most of my clients are in Australia and the UK. I work remotely and adjust my availability for your time zone.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?

Every site I build is fully responsive and tested on mobile, tablet and desktop. Speed optimization is part of my process, not an extra.

How long does a website take?

It depends on scope. A landing page can be ready within a week, while a full business website or online store usually takes 1–3 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief about your business and goals, your design or reference websites, and your content (text, logo, images). I'll guide you through the rest.
