FAQ

Common questions

Have another question? Just ask me.

Which platforms do you work with?

Mainly WordPress (Elementor Pro, Divi, Gutenberg, WPBakery), WooCommerce and Shopify. I also work with GoHighLevel, Wix and Squarespace.

Can you build my website from a Figma or HTML design?

Yes. Figma and HTML to WordPress is one of my core services. Send me your Figma, XD, PSD or HTML file and I'll build a pixel-perfect, responsive website from it.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?

Every site I build is fully responsive and tested on mobile, tablet and desktop. Speed optimization is part of my process, not an extra.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes 1–2 days, a business or services website about 1 week, and an online store 1–2 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief about your business and goals, your design or reference websites, and your content (text, logo, images). I'll guide you through the rest.
